## Formal Status

The baseline intentionally distinguishes between definitions, hypotheses, assumptions, conditional theorems, and empirical metrics.

In particular:

- H1 Prefix Stability remains a hypothesis unless established for a specified trajectory class.
- H2 Family Separability remains an assumption/bounded empirical condition unless established for a specified family set.
- Search-space reduction theorems are conditional on the stated relationship between admissible-space measure and search effort.
- Any claim of \(p<0.001\), a specific \(\epsilon\), or a specific reduction factor \(R\) requires supporting experimental data.

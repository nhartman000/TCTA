# Transform Algebra and Trajectory-Constrained Transform Algebra

**Nicholas Hartman / American Milestone Inc.**

This repository holds the mathematical and computational specifications. The proposed physical and ontological account belongs in **Theory of Intelligence — An Ontological Abstract**, maintained separately. The formal definitions and conditional results here do not depend on observer, membrane, awareness or intelligence claims.

## Formal volumes

- [Volume I — State, Transform and Admissible Trajectory](docs/VOLUME_I_FORMAL_FOUNDATIONS.md): state representations, transforms, trajectories, constraints, CIIU and branch preservation. General equivalence and inverse results remain open.
- [Volume II — Prefix Resolution and Conditional Reduction](docs/VOLUME_II_TCTA_PREFIX_REDUCTION.md): Γ, H1/H2, OGSI Ψ, conditional reduction, evaluation and research limits. The exact classifier and thresholds still require a frozen implementation before blinded evaluation.
